# Privacy policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026

## Who operates this service
Launchcheck is operated by Sayed Zahur. For support, privacy requests or payment questions, contact the operator through https://github.com/SayedZahur786. Do not post sensitive information in public issues; use the contact methods on that profile to arrange a private exchange.

## Information and purpose
We process the public website or repository URL you submit, scan timing, status, score and check evidence. We fetch a bounded sample of public pages or public GitHub source files to produce the report. Source files are processed transiently and not stored verbatim. Do not submit confidential URLs, credentials or personal information. A hashed network identifier is used to limit abuse. Payment records contain a Stripe session identifier and report identifier; we never receive your full payment-card number.

## Cookies and analytics
An essential, HTTP-only browser cookie proves access to reports you create. It expires after 30 days, uses SameSite=Lax, and is secure on the hosted HTTPS service. There are no advertising cookies or enabled third-party analytics. The shared page-view snippet is disabled. Operational counts of completed scans and verified purchases help assess the service. Stripe may use its own necessary cookies on its hosted checkout page.

## Service providers and transfers
Vercel hosts the application. Neon stores scan records and payment references in a dedicated PostgreSQL project. Stripe processes payments when checkout is enabled. GitHub serves public repository metadata and source files during repository scans. These providers may process information outside your country under their own terms and safeguards. No private repository access is requested.

## Retention and security
Browser access expires after 30 days; this does not automatically delete a report. Scan records are retained until deletion is requested or the service is retired. Payment references are retained as needed for refunds, accounting and dispute handling. Rate-limit identifiers are short-lived hourly buckets, with expired rows removed during maintenance. Public summary links are readable by anyone who has the link. Full evidence requires the creating browser and a verified payment. We use access checks, bounded requests and encrypted provider connections, but cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Your choices and rights
You can stop using the service at any time and clear its cookies. Clearing cookies may remove access to purchased evidence. For access, correction or deletion requests, contact the operator and provide the report link without publishing sensitive evidence. We may ask for proof of control before changing records. Public report links should be shared carefully. Rights and retention obligations depend on applicable law; these terms do not remove statutory rights.

## Changes
We will publish material changes on this page with a revised effective date. Review this policy before submitting a new scan. If a change requires consent under applicable law, we will request it before enabling the relevant processing.
